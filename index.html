Mr. Mothi.R, M.Com., B.Ed., DAT., is the Founder & Director of Dwarka Tuition Centre, Chennai, established in 2018.

With a passion for teaching and student development, he has been guiding students towards academic success through quality education, individual attention, and disciplined coaching.

Under his leadership, Dwarka Tuition Centre has earned the trust of students and parents by achieving excellent academic results and creating a positive learning environment.
